My GitHub activity reflects this momentum, with 300+ yearly contributions and multiple public repositories connected to creative coding, AI workflows, teaching, animation, design systems, and emerging world-model research. This page represents how I use AI not simply to generate code, but to extend my creative direction, production experience, and technical imagination into working software.

Part Smart Innovation Platform

I developed Part Smart as an integrated product-education and creative-technology platform for MPA. The video here just represents one aspect of that effort, which was the video production pipeline I ran and developed. Key projects included Install Assist—a React support system that replaced printed instructions and saved more than $500,000—along with a Blackmagic–Quantum–CatDV media pipeline, automated 360° product-imaging tools, interactive kiosks, WebGL product experiences, AR/VR training, and scalable web workflows. I also prototyped an AI-powered video knowledgebase, internal RAG tools, predictive applications, 3D-scanning systems, and automotive digital-twin research.
